Any iteration that was in progress when the interrupt was raised is partially applied: some of `tail_events`, `tail_opencode`, `scan_bugs` may have completed, but `live.update` might not have been called with the final layout, or `time.sleep` might not have finished. The live display showed the layout that was last generated. After the interrupt is caught, no further modifications to `state` or the display occur. If an exception other than `KeyboardInterrupt` arises at any point (during argument parsing, `State` initialization, the `Live` setup, or within the loop), that exception propagates out of the function; before it propagates, the `Live` context manager’s `__exit__` method is invoked, stopping the live display and restoring the terminal.
